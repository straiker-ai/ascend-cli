Ascend CLI // Architecture
THE AGENTIC SECURITY COMPANY

The CLI runs on your machine. It authenticates to the Straiker Console with your PAT, builds an adapter that speaks each customer target's protocol, registers the target as an app, and, during a run, automatically starts a bridge that relays Ascend's probes to the target and the results back. Click any component inside the CLI for its commands.
